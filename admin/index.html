Welcome to the Keepers’ Room

This is where we hold the people side of the village: who’s joining, what they’re drawn to, and who’s doing what. A shared map of our crew, so it doesn’t all live in one person’s head. It’s yours as much as ours.

The two views

Timeline — everyone who’s said yes, newest first, with when they applied and the roles they picked.

By role — the same people, grouped by role. Flip between what they chose (their pick on the form) and what you assigned (what we’ve actually given them) to watch the crew take shape.

Opening someone

Click any card to see their full answers: why they’re here, what they’d love to offer, dietary and access needs, and more.

Giving someone a role

  • Set a status — Confirmed, Maybe, Waitlist, or Not this time.
  • Assign roles and activities (free text, with suggestions).
  • Leave private notes — only the crew sees these, never the applicant.
  • Hit Save. That’s it.

How it stays honest

The roster syncs from the website signup form automatically, so new yeses just appear. Your assignments live separately and are never overwritten by a sync. Edit freely, nothing you do here gets clobbered.

The seven roles

Working descriptions, refine as you like.

  • Fire Keeper — tends the warmth, holds the fire and the closing circles.
  • Story Weaver — gathers people’s stories and gives them back to the village.
  • Basecamp Steward — the beating heart of the site: coordination, logistics, who’s-where. (Savannah & Alex, that’s you.)
  • Threshold Keeper — welcomes arrivals and helps people land softly.
  • Exchange Guide — helps people find what to give and what to receive.
  • Flow Facilitator — keeps the rhythm of the days: sessions, music, movement.
  • Not sure yet — still finding their place. Welcome here too.

Want something changed?

This is built to make your job lighter. If something’s clunky, or you want a new view or a tweak, just say, Heenal genuinely loves making this better.
