Assumption of risk

The Organisers have taken all reasonable precautions to make spaces on the Land safe for facilitators and participants. However, facilitation and participation in any outdoor activity is potentially dangerous. You may be exposed to risks of property loss or damage and personal injury while on the Land.

We ask that you accept all risks and responsibility for any physical injury to yourself, damage to your property, or other losses to you of any kind, which might occur while you are on the Land, regardless of whether such risks are connected in any way with your participation in any activities offered at The Living Village.

I, ___________________________, hereby affirm that I have been well advised of the inherent risks associated with visiting the Land and taking part in The Living Village. I understand those risks include, but are not limited to:

  • exposure to potentially poisonous plants, insects and wildlife
  • uneven and slippery walking surfaces, and unlit paths after dark
  • open fire at the fire circle, and the handling of firewood
  • a pond on the Land
  • camping outdoors overnight, and exposure to weather
  • outhouses and a cold outdoor shower rather than indoor plumbing
  • no drinking water from the taps
  • shared preparation and consumption of food, including food brought by other participants
  • physical work outdoors, including any activity in which participants give back to the Land

and other risks which may result in property damage, personal injury or even death.

I understand that injuries can occur even where the Organisers have taken all reasonable precautions. I further understand that due to the rural location of The Living Centre and the limited mobile phone signal there, medical treatment for such injuries could be delayed. Nonetheless, I choose to attend The Living Village and to facilitate and participate in the spaces and activities that it offers.

I hereby personally assume all risks which may occur while at The Living Centre for any harm, injury, illness, death or damage that may befall me as a result of visiting The Living Centre and attending, facilitating or participating in The Living Village, whether foreseen or unforeseen. I wish to attend and to facilitate and participate in its activities in spite of those risks and the possible delay in medical assistance.

I agree that I will assume all responsibility for any injury, illness or damage and related costs, medical expenses and damages of any kind that I may incur arising out of or in any way related to visiting The Living Centre or the activities offered there.

I further agree that I hold The Institute for Community Sustainability, The Vibe Collective and The Living Centre, their owners, officers, agents, managers, contractors, employees, volunteers and assigns free of any and all claims for liability, injury, illness, death, costs, medical bills or other damages incurred by me or my family, heirs or assigns, no matter how caused, including damages, injuries, illness or death alleged to be partially or solely caused by their omissions, commissions, carelessness, negligence or liability without fault.

I expressly do not give up the rights and remedies that may be available to me under Canadian law, which may arise due to the gross negligence or wilful misconduct of any of the Organisers.

I understand that this waiver and release of liability applies whether or not such injury, damage or loss is in any way connected with the facilitation of or participation in any activities offered on the Land, and whether such injuries result from my own negligent acts or from the acts of other participants. I also understand these terms are contractual and not a mere recital. I have signed this document of my own free will.

I further agree that in the event of any dispute whatsoever between me and any of the Organisers concerning my facilitation of or participation in The Living Village, I will first initiate a fair and equitable alternative dispute resolution procedure such as mediation.

Care for each other and the Land

I understand that The Living Village is a shared, co-created gathering and that I hold personal responsibility for myself, for others and for the Land. I agree to follow the site rules I have been given, which include:

  • no swimming in the pond
  • crossing the bridges and entering the forest are not permitted
  • no smoking, vaping, alcohol or other substances on the property
  • quiet hours between 10pm and 8am
  • no food left in tents, the straw bale area or the cabins
  • following the instructions of the Organisers and their volunteers, including in an emergency

I will let the Organisers know of any allergy, medical condition or access need that they should be aware of in an emergency.
