Participant Agreement

Please read this before you arrive, then sign at the bottom. It takes about two minutes. Everyone coming onto the land needs to sign, including day visitors and crew.

Where we are gathering

The Living Village takes place at The Living Centre, 5871 Bells Rd, London, Ontario, on private rural land, hosted by Journeyland (a program of the Institute for Community Sustainability) together with The Vibe Collective.

What I understand about the land

This is a working piece of countryside, not a serviced venue. I understand that it includes:

  • uneven ground, long grass, tree roots, and paths without lighting after dark
  • ponds and other water on the property
  • open fire, and cooking equipment
  • outdoor camping, outhouses, and an outdoor cold-water shower
  • weather that can change quickly, including heat, storms, wind and rain
  • insects and ticks, and wild animals
  • tap water that is not drinkable. Drinking water is only at the marked water stations
  • patchy mobile phone reception, and a distance from the nearest hospital

I accept that being outdoors in a place like this carries risk that cannot be fully removed, and I am choosing to take part anyway.

Looking after myself and each other

  • I take responsibility for my own choices, my own body, and my own belongings.
  • I will stay within the marked event areas. Crossing the bridges and entering the forest are not permitted. The Stone People Pond is for looking at only, no swimming.
  • This is a sober space. No alcohol, smoking, vaping or other substances on the property.
  • I will respect quiet hours between 10pm and 8am, for the sake of our neighbours.
  • I will store my food in sealed containers, and leave no trace of my stay.
  • If I am bringing a child, they remain my responsibility at all times, and I will supervise them.
  • I will follow reasonable directions from the organisers, especially about fire, water and safety.
  • I will treat everyone here with care and respect. Harassment of any kind means being asked to leave.

Release of liability

In exchange for being allowed to take part, I agree that I take part at my own risk. To the fullest extent permitted by Ontario law, I release Journeyland, the Institute for Community Sustainability, The Vibe Collective, The Living Centre, the owners of the land, and their organisers, facilitators, staff and volunteers from claims for injury, illness, loss or damage to property arising from my taking part, except where it is caused by their own gross negligence or deliberate wrongdoing.

I confirm I am 18 or older, or that I am the parent or legal guardian signing on behalf of the child or children named below.

Medical

I confirm I am well enough to take part. If I cannot speak for myself in an emergency, I consent to the organisers arranging first aid or medical care for me, and I accept responsibility for the cost of any treatment or transport.

Photography and film

This gathering is being documented on camera. Unless I opt out below, I agree that photographs and video that include me may be used by Journeyland and the Institute for Community Sustainability to share the story of the Living Village, without payment to me. If I opt out, I will let the crew and the videographer know on arrival so they can avoid filming me.

Privacy

The details I give here are used for running this event safely, including emergency contact and any medical needs. They are not sold or shared beyond the organising crew.
